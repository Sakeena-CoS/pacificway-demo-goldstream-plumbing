Our Services

Every plumbing problem, solved.

From a dripping tap to a full renovation — Goldstream Plumbing handles it all with transparency, expertise, and a fully stocked van.

Emergency Plumbing
Burst pipes, flooding, no hot water — we respond fast, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Don't let a plumbing emergency damage your home any further.
Drain Clearing
Slow or blocked drains cleared with professional equipment. From kitchen sinks to main sewer lines, we diagnose and fix the root cause — not just the symptom.
Water Heaters
Tankless and traditional water heater installation, repair, and replacement. We work with all major brands and can advise on the most energy-efficient solution for your home.
Gas Lines
Licensed gas fitting for appliances, stoves, fireplaces, and outdoor BBQ lines. Safety-tested and certified — never attempt gas work without a licensed contractor.
Pipe Repair
Leaking, corroded, or frozen pipes repaired or replaced with minimal disruption. We use quality materials built to last for decades in BC's climate.
Renovations
Kitchen and bathroom plumbing for renovations — rough-in, fixture installation, and finish work. Fully coordinated with your contractor or managed start to finish.

Why Choose Goldstream

Langford's trusted plumbers.

  • Transparent pricing — we quote up front so you always know what you're paying before work begins.
  • Fully stocked vans — most jobs completed in a single visit because we carry the parts we need.

  • Family-owned & community-focused — we live and work in Langford and take care of our neighbours.
